Using the earning registers

Enter TOM & CC and TVM figures in their own editable tables. Review the calculated totals in the read-only Final Earning page for the selected business date.

Offline entries are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. The application will show synchronization status when the station server is configured.

Support contact details can be added when provided.
